2. Access Control: Unauthorized access to this system is strictly prohibited and monitored. All access attempts, including IP addresses and metadata, are logged and audited regularly to prevent breaches.

4. Third-Party Sharing: Your medical requests are strictly confidential and shared only with verified government and private medical institutions within Maharashtra as required to fulfill your requests.
